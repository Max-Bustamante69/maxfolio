## What was real, and what I fixed

### 1. The "How it ships" pinned readout could show a numeral, "Step n of 5" and a title from three
different steps at once — **fixed**, `src/components/sections/Process.tsx`

This is the shared `Process` component every theme (Apple/Neo/Luxury/Brutalist, and Arcade too as a
side effect) mounts for its "how it ships" section. The pinned column on wide screens has three
pieces that must always name the same step: a 150px numeral, a "Step N of 5" label, and the step
title. Reproduced with **real wheel-scroll input** (not `scrollTo()` — see the harness's
`checkProcessReadout`/the standalone wheel-scroll probe used to verify this), stepping through all
5 steps twice: **8 genuine mismatches** on the unfixed code, e.g. numeral "05" next to "Step 4 of 5"
next to the title "Entrega", or numeral "01" next to a title still reading "QA" (the owner's exact
report).

Root causes, both timing bugs in the crossfades:

- The numeral's `AnimatePresence` had no `mode="wait"` — the outgoing and incoming numeral could
  render simultaneously mid-crossfade, so a frame in that window has *two* numerals in the DOM and a
  querySelector (or a glance) can land on the wrong one relative to the title next to it.
- The "Step N of 5" label was **plain, unanimated text** — it snapped to the new value the instant
  `active` changed, while the numeral and title were still mid-exit on the *old* value (their exit
  animations run to completion regardless of `prefers-reduced-motion`, since only their `initial` is
  gated). And even after giving it its own crossfade, a *faster* one (0.3s/0.1s, matching the title)
  still settled on the new step before the numeral (0.45s/0.25s) did — same bug, smaller window.

Fix: all three now share one `key={active}` crossfade cycle, `mode="wait"`, and **identical**
enter/exit durations (0.45s / 0.25s, matching the numeral — the numeral is the visual anchor, so the
other two were sped up to meet it rather than blunting the numeral's reveal). Re-verified with the
same real-wheel-scroll probe: **0 mismatches** across a full pass through all 5 steps at both a normal
and a deliberately fast scroll speed (80 samples, 0 mismatches).

### 2. Sticky itself — investigated, **not actually broken**

The task brief named "an overflow-hidden ancestor" as the usual cause of a broken sticky readout, and
every theme's root wrapper does carry an `overflow-x-hidden` / `[overflow-x:clip]` class (for item
(d), horizontal-overflow protection) — which, per the CSS Overflow spec, does force the other axis
from `visible` to `auto`, a documented sticky-breaking footgun. I built and ran that fix (moving the
guard to `html` in `src/styles/index.css`, removing the per-page classes), then measured **before
committing to it**: with **real wheel-scroll input**, `position: sticky` engaged correctly (constant
`top: 112px` across 9 consecutive samples) on the *original, unmodified* code too. The apparent
break only showed up when I drove the scroll with direct `window.scrollTo()` calls, which fight
Lenis's own rAF-driven smoothing loop (`lerp: 0.1`) and produce a scroll position that never
settles where the test expects — a harness artifact, not a real one. I reverted the overflow-x
change (kept only the verified fix above) rather than ship an unnecessary refactor across five pages
on the strength of a misleading test.

What the owner actually saw was almost certainly fix #1 — a numeral/title/step-label that don't
agree reads as "the readout is broken", independent of whether the box is literally pinned.

### 3. "Giant blank spaces" — measured, mostly not bugs; one real methodology trap found and fixed
**in the harness**

Every route's section-to-section gaps land in the 150–350px range on both breakpoints, matching this
design system's own `py-16/20/24/28/32/36` section-padding scale — consistent, not broken. The
`/luxury` and `/brutalist` hero→next gaps (582–955px) trace to a `min-h-screen` hero with vertically
centered content, which is a deliberate full-bleed hero treatment on both themes, not a collapsed
element — plus, on mobile, the `Ticker`'s `prefers-reduced-motion` static-list fallback (a real,
visible list of ~20 "brand · role" pairs, each too wide to share a row on a 390px viewport) sitting
between the two sections. I tried tightening that list (letting item text wrap) and it measured
*taller*, not shorter, so I reverted rather than ship a guess — and on reflection this isn't the
reported defect anyway: it's real, readable content, not a dead area (my own "oversized-empty"
detector correctly does not flag it, since it requires no visible text/media inside). Recorded as an
open observation below, not fixed.

The one real bug was in my *first* pass at measuring gaps, not the site: headings and body copy
render through `<RevealText>`, whose words sit at `opacity: 0` until a scroll-triggered
`whileInView` fires — reading geometry immediately after `goto()`, before anything below the fold had
been scrolled into view, made every not-yet-revealed heading invisible to a content scan and hugely
inflated the measured gap (one read as 841px; the same boundary reads 277px once revealed). Fixed by
measuring gaps/oversized-empty under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (a second, dedicated page per
route/viewport) — the app already renders `RevealText` in its settled, visible state under that
preference (`initial={reduced ? false : {...}}`), so this reads the same resting layout a scrolled-to
visitor sees, without needing to scripted-scroll through the whole page first.

### 4. Everything else: clean

- **Scroll stalls (a):** 0 real stalls on every route at both breakpoints, wheel-driven at 1440 and
  touch-drag-driven at 390 (Menu's original "1 stall" was the harness clamping scrollY at the
  document's natural bottom and mistaking "can't scroll further" for jank — fixed by excluding
  samples pinned within 4px of the top/bottom of the scrollable range).
- **Horizontal overflow @390 (d):** none on any route.
- **Console errors (e):** clean except Apple's `/api/ab` 404 ×2 (desktop+mobile) — that endpoint is a
  Vercel serverless function (`recordAb()`'s first-party counter, explicitly documented as
  best-effort in `src/ab.ts`) that doesn't exist under the local Vite dev server; it resolves once
  deployed and isn't wired to anything user-visible. Not a real defect, and out of scope for a static
  QA pass against `localhost`.
- **Overlaps (f):** 0 on every theme this lane owns. Arcade showed one `NAV` vs. a `pointer-events-none`
  full-bleed decorative background layer (`-z-10`) — harmless by construction (non-interactive, behind
  everything) and excluded from the harness's overlap check once I scoped it to elements that can
  actually compete for a click.
- **Dark mode (g):** toggles correctly on every route. (First pass under-read `document.body`'s
  background, which none of these pages ever change — the dark/light class lives on each theme's own
  `[role="document"]` root wrapper — so it read "false" everywhere; fixed in the harness.)
- **Theme switcher (h):** Apple is reachable from a visitor pinned to `neo` via the Explore-grid link
  at the bottom of the page (`d.href` = `/?v=apple`); lands on `.theme-apple`. The only wrinkle was the
  harness's own wait — `TransitionLink`'s curtain animation defers the actual route change to 1200ms
  after the click (`PageTransition.tsx`), so an 800ms wait made it look like navigation never happened.

## Open observations (not fixed, not the reported bugs)

- `/luxury` and `/brutalist`: under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` on a narrow (390px) viewport, the
  `Ticker` component's static fallback list (`~20` items, up to ~350px wide each, `whitespace-nowrap`)
  renders essentially one item per row and measures ~760px tall between the hero and the next section.
  Real, readable content — not blank space — but tall enough to feel like dead scroll on a phone with
  Reduce Motion on. Worth a follow-up pass on `.ticker-static-wrap` sizing specifically for that
  accessibility path; a first attempt (letting item text wrap) made it taller, not shorter, so it
  needs real design input rather than a guess.
- The harness's generic `checkSticky()` (used for anything other than the Process readout) still
  drives its probe scroll with `window.scrollTo()`, which §2 above showed can misreport under Lenis.
  It returned 0 broken stickies on every route across every run, which is consistent with what the
  real-wheel probe found for Process, but it hasn't been independently re-verified with real wheel
  input for every other `position: sticky` element on the site the way Process was — flag as a
  known limitation, not a known bug.
