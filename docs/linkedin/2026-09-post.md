Most Shopify stores I audit don't have a traffic problem. They have a leak.

The ads work. People land. Then the page takes 5 seconds on a phone, the offer is a bare price, and
the bundle discount lives in a coupon nobody finds. The order never happens — and the dashboard
blames the ads.

Here's what I do about it, store by store:

→ Speed: real Core Web Vitals on mobile, not a desktop score.
→ Offers that do the math for the shopper: tiered bundles, quantity breaks, free-gift thresholds —
computed by Shopify Functions, not coupon codes.
→ A/B tests you can actually read: Bayesian, sample-ratio checked, one clear winner.
→ Checkout friction removed before you spend another dollar on ads.

I've done this across 18+ Shopify storefronts as CTO at Digitdeck, with our own app suite
(A/B testing, bundles, reviews, back in stock) running the mechanics.
