### minimal/apple
Type: one confident sans (SF Pro/Inter-alike) at huge display sizes, generous tracking, little else.
Color: near-white or near-black ground, a single ink color, at most one accent used sparingly.
Grid: wide centered column, huge negative-space margins, content rarely exceeds ~60% viewport width.
Motion: slow fades/opacity, gentle parallax, no more than one moving element on screen at a time.
Imagery: a handful of large, color-corrected product/portrait photos; never a busy collage.

### editorial/magazine
Type: serif or serif+sans pairing, pull-quotes, drop caps, asymmetric headline sizing like a print spread.
Color: paper-toned neutrals, ink black, one editorial accent (often red or a muted jewel tone).
Grid: multi-column magazine grid, text wraps around images, generous kerning, footnote-style captions.
Motion: page-turn/scroll-reveal of columns, subtle image parallax, cursor-following captions.
Imagery: art-directed photography treated as content, not decoration — full-bleed spreads, tight crops.

### swiss/grid
Type: Helvetica/Akzidenz-adjacent grotesk, strict baseline grid, left-aligned, numbered sections.
Color: red/black/white or a restrained 2-color system, high contrast, no gradients.
Grid: visible or implied modular grid, consistent column widths, alignment is the whole aesthetic.
Motion: functional only — grid lines draw in, numbers count up, no ornamental animation.
Imagery: rare, and when present cropped to the grid; diagrams and data tables carry more weight than photos.

### brutalist
Type: raw system fonts or oversized serif, unstyled default `<a>` blue links left in on purpose.
Color: harsh black/white, sometimes a jarring single neon accent; no gradients.
Grid: intentionally broken/overlapping layout, no consistent margins, text can overflow the viewport.
Motion: abrupt, unsmoothed — instant color-invert on hover, no easing, glitch/jitter over polish.
Imagery: low-fi, unretouched, sometimes scanned textures or ASCII in place of a photo.

### neo-brutalist
Type: bold rounded/chunky sans (often a variable font), big and playful rather than raw.
Color: saturated flat primary/candy colors, thick black outlines, hard drop-shadows (no blur).
Grid: card-based, deliberately off-grid tilt/rotation on individual cards, visible borders everywhere.
Motion: snappy spring/bounce on hover (press-down + shadow-offset shift), sticker-style micro-interactions.
Imagery: flat illustration or 3D-toy renders over photography; stickers, badges, hand-drawn arrows.

### terminal/hacker + comic/manga
Terminal: monospace type, green/amber-on-black or pure black/white CRT palette, blinking cursor,
  command-line framing (`$`, `>`, ASCII borders), scanline/CRT-flicker motion, screenshots framed
  as terminal windows rather than photos.
Comic/manga: bold outlined type in speech-bubble/panel frames, halftone-dot shading, primary +
  black ink palette, panel-grid layout with gutters, "impact"-style motion (screen-shake, speed
  lines, onomatopoeia pop-ins), hand-inked/cel-shaded imagery over photography.
