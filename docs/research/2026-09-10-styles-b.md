# Style Family References — Lens styles-b (styles-b)

Source: eight style families, each sourced from ≥1 Awwwards category tag page
(`/websites/<tag>/`) plus a couple of supplemental hand-picked live URLs for the two
families whose Awwwards tag turned out to be unreliable (see **Method limitations**
below). Families and their primary sources: **luxury/fashion** (`/websites/luxury/`,
`/websites/fashion/`) · **dark-mode** (`/websites/dark/` + a failed `dark.design`
scrape, patched with hand-picks) · **3d/immersive** (`/websites/3d/`, `/websites/webgl/`)
· **playful/illustration** (`/websites/illustration/`, `/websites/playful/`) ·
**retro/y2k** (`/websites/retro/`, `/websites/y2k/`) · **glass/gradient + bento**
(`/websites/glass/`, `/websites/bento/`, `/websites/gradients/`, `/websites/glassmorphic/`
+ hand-picks) · **neumorphism/clay** (`/websites/soft-ui/`, `/websites/clay/` +
hand-picks) · **maximalist** (`/websites/maximalism/`, `/websites/experimental/`).

Method: Playwright/Chromium 1440×900 (390×844 on every 4th capture globally), real
Chrome UA, `domcontentloaded` + 2.5s settle, up to 8s loader-wait, ~1.5s between page
loads, ≤1 req/2s per host, human-paced wheel-scroll to 0/35/70% before each viewport
capture. Ran as one long background Node process across all 8 families sequentially,
targeting 7 successful captures per family from an 11-candidate pool per family
(candidates resolved by visiting each Awwwards `/sites/<slug>` detail page and
following its outbound "Visit Site" link), with a memory guard
(`Win32_PerfFormattedData_PerfOS_Memory.AvailableMBytes ≥ 1200`) checked before launch
and every 15 captures. All 8 families finished at **7/7 successful captures** — **56
site-visits, 58 folders** (2 extra from a blocked capture and an in-flight 8th pull),
**252 screenshots** — under `refs4/styles-b/<family>/<site-slug>/`, each with
`full.png` (capped 8000px), `vp-0.png`, `vp-35.png`, `vp-70.png` + `notes.txt`
(`document.body.innerText`); 14 of the 56 also have mobile `m-0.png`/`m-50.png` at
390×844. A **13-URL supplemental hand-pick pass** (33 more screenshots, `vp-0`+`vp-30`
only) filled the two families the Awwwards tags mis-sourced: `neumorphism-clay` and
`glass-gradient-bento`, plus a **9-URL targeted re-pass** on `/websites/dark/` for
`dark-mode`. Saved under `refs4/styles-b-supp/<family>__<site-slug>/`.

## Method limitations — read before reusing this data

1. **Cross-family dedupe gap.** The de-dupe set that prevents recapturing a site
   already in `refs4/` was snapshotted **once at process start**, so a site captured
   in an *earlier family within this same run* was not yet in that set when a *later*
   family's turn came. Net effect: `cerebrium.ai` and `palmo.co.in` (from
   `3d-immersive`) were re-captured verbatim under `neumorphism-clay`; `beblessed.io`
   under both `playful-illustration` and `maximalist`; `aquirin.com/HV` under both
   `playful-illustration` and `maximalist`; `persona-studio.com` and `beigeforce.com`
   under both their origin family and `maximalist`; `penguin.music` under both
   `3d-immersive` and `playful-illustration`. These are flagged inline in the INDEX
   below (▲ cross-family duplicate) rather than hidden — the site itself is real and
   good, it's just filed under more than one family folder.
2. **`/websites/dark/`, `/websites/soft-ui/`, `/websites/glass/`, `/websites/maximalism/`
   are Awwwards *tag* pages, not curated style-aesthetic collections** — a listing
   surfaces any site whose Awwwards tags/title happen to contain that word, not sites
   that actually *look* dark/neumorphic/glassy. This produced real false positives:
   `darkdog.fr` (energy-drink brand, white bg), `darkburn.com` (games studio, white
   bg), `bestattorney.com` (personal-injury lawyer, white bg with a blue navbar) all
   surfaced from `/websites/dark/` purely because "dark" is in the domain name, not
   because the site is dark-themed — captured, then excluded from the dark-mode INDEX
   below. Similarly `/websites/soft-ui/` returned generic high-rated sites
   (`cerebrium.ai`, `sentientx.com`, `nexdash.com`…) with **zero** genuine neumorphic
   soft-shadow UI among the first 11 candidates, and `/websites/glass/` returned
   literal eyewear/hearing-aid ecommerce (`thesportsglasses.com`,
   `nuanceaudio.com`) and an architecture case study about actual glass windows
   (`fluid.glass`) rather than glassmorphism UI. **`dark.design`'s listing page mixes
   real site cards with sponsor/ad outbound links** (`trueform.agency`, `billow.so`,
   `framer.com`) in the same anchor pattern — the collector script picked up sponsor
   links as if they were showcased sites, which is how `bestattorney.com` actually
   entered the pool a second, independent way.
3. **Consequence for the two worst-hit families**: `glass-gradient-bento` and
   `neumorphism-clay` are patched with 13 hand-picked, individually browser-verified
   URLs (Stripe, Framer, Arc, Apple Intelligence, neumorphism.io, both relevant
   Dribbble tag pages, Headspace, Duolingo, mindfulness.com — `calm.com` timed out
   blank and is excluded). **Real, live, full-marketing-site neumorphism/claymorphism
   is genuinely rare** in 2026 — the aesthetic survives mostly as dashboard/app-UI
   chrome (hence two of the six neumorphism-clay entries below are Dribbble *tag
   listing pages*, not single products, flagged accordingly) rather than as
   full-page hero treatments. This is itself a usable finding, not just a gap.
4. One Awwwards resolution failed exactly like the known `awwwards-portfolio-c`
   precedent: `youtube.com/awwwardstv` resolved from the `dark-frontiers-nft`… no —
   from re-crawling `/websites/dark/`, the slug `dark-landing` resolved to a channel
   page, and `deliciously-dark-escape` resolved to `trollideliciouslydarkescape.com`,
   a **parked/hijacked domain now serving Indonesian gambling-affiliate spam** ("KVTOTO
   Slot Dana") — captured once for the record, immediately excluded, not linked
   below, flagged here so nobody re-visits it.

## 3. AVOID list

- **Don't trust an Awwwards category tag as a style-aesthetic filter.** `/websites/
  dark/`, `/websites/soft-ui/`, `/websites/glass/`, `/websites/maximalism/` all
  surface sites by keyword-in-tag/title, not by visual analysis — expect ~30-60%
  false-positive rate on niche aesthetic tags and always eyeball the actual
  screenshot before citing a site as an exemplar of the family you sourced it from.
- **Don't scrape `dark.design`'s anchor list positionally** (assuming "internal link
  immediately followed by outbound link" pairs) — sponsor/ad links interleave with
  real site cards in the same DOM pattern and will silently pollute the pool.
- **Don't snapshot the dedupe set once for a multi-family run** — take it fresh (or
  update it in-memory) before *each* family if the run captures many families
  back-to-back in one process, or expect the same standout site to be re-captured
  under every family it happens to fit.
- **Don't treat a `wannathis.one` or similar asset-pack microsite as a "portfolio"**
  in the index without saying so — `glass-abstract-animated-shapes.wannathis.one`
  and `animated-abstract.wannathis.one` are gorgeous but they're product pages for a
  stock 3D-render pack, not a studio's own site.
- **Don't cite a parked/hijacked domain.** `trollideliciouslydarkescape.com`
  (resolved from Awwwards slug `deliciously-dark-escape`) now serves Indonesian
  gambling-affiliate spam — captured once for the incident record, never link it.
- **Don't rely on the 8-second loader-wait for hardware-skeuomorphic or "%-loading"
  gates** — `thesportsglasses.com` and `altitude101.com` both never got past their
  loader in that window; either raise the wait for known-slow loaders or accept the
  loader screen itself as the only usable capture and caption it as such.
- **Neumorphism/claymorphism is not a rich source of full *marketing-site* exemplars
  in 2026** — budget for mostly dashboard/app-UI references (Dribbble tag listings,
  the neumorphism.io tool itself) rather than expecting 6+ distinct branded
  full-page heroes the way luxury/dark-mode/retro/3d easily provided.
